## 🏆 Elite Open-Source & Fellowship Programs

These programs are highly competitive but offer some of the best learning experiences available globally. Most are paid and fully remote.

| Program | What It Offers |
|---------|---------------|
| [Google Summer of Code (GSoC)](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) | Paid stipend to contribute to open-source organizations under mentorship. One of the most prestigious programs for CS students globally. |
| [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/) | 12-week remote internship alternative. You build real production software and contribute to open-source under expert mentorship. |
| [Linux Foundation Mentorship](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/) | Structured mentorship on open-source projects across cloud, security, and blockchain technologies. |
| [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) | Paid remote open-source internships offered twice a year. Specifically for people from underrepresented groups in tech. |

> ✦ GSoC and MLH Fellowship are highly regarded on resumes worldwide. They are worth applying to even if the acceptance rate is low.

## ⚠️ Challenges to Be Aware Of

**Competition is global**
- The global internship market is significantly more competitive than domestic platforms
- You are competing with students from top universities worldwide, not just your city or country

**Visa and employment regulations**
- Many companies, especially in the US and Europe, cannot legally hire international interns without visa sponsorship
- Always check eligibility requirements before investing time in an application

**Remote international roles are harder than they appear**
- Fully paid remote internships at foreign companies are rare for international students
- Companies often prefer local candidates for tax, timezone, and compliance reasons

**✦ What actually works**
- GSoC, MLH Fellowship, Outreachy, and Linux Foundation Mentorship are your best bets for legitimate, paid, remote global experience
- Wellfound and LinkedIn give direct access to startup founders who are more flexible about remote international hires
- Build a strong public GitHub profile. Many global opportunities come through open-source visibility, not job applications
